REFUND POLICY

Customers may request a refund when they have been incorrectly charged, charged for a cancelled service, charged twice, or charged an amount that does not match the agreed invoice.

Refund requests should include the invoice number, customer details, reason for the refund, and relevant supporting information.

The service provider may review the transaction before approving a refund.

Additional documentation may be requested when the reason for the refund cannot be verified using the available billing information.

Approved refunds are normally processed within seven business days after the refund request has been approved.

If an invoice was paid using an electronic payment method, the refund will normally be returned using the original payment method.

Refunds may take additional time to appear in the customer's account depending on the payment provider.

A refund does not automatically cancel an outstanding invoice.

Any remaining undisputed amount must still be paid according to the applicable payment terms.

Duplicate payments may be eligible for a refund after the duplicate transaction has been verified.

If an incorrect invoice was paid, the service provider may issue a corrected invoice, credit, or refund depending on the circumstances.

Customers should retain confirmation of an approved refund for their records.

If a refund has been approved but has not been received after the expected processing period, the customer should contact the service provider and provide the relevant invoice or refund reference.
